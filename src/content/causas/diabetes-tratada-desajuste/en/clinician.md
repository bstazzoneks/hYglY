## Clinical summary
Review dose, food, activity and absorption.
## Definition
Iatrogenic hypoglycemia from temporal mismatch.
## Epidemiology
Common with insulin or secretagogue treatment.
## Pathophysiology
Medicine action exceeds glucose availability.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial or late according to medicine.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple's triad and timing correlation.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Sulfonylurea screen | Excludes exposure |
| Gastric emptying | Assesses gastroparesis |

## Differential diagnosis
Dumping, insulinoma, alcohol and factitious hypoglycemia.
## Management
Adjust treatment and safety education.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and driving contraindication.
## Follow-up
Monitor timing, dose and glucose.
## Special populations
Pregnancy, pediatric patients and older adults.
