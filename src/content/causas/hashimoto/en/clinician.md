## Clinical summary
Relate hypoglycemia to associated endocrine deficit, not isolated thyroiditis.
## Definition
Autoimmune thyroid disease with possible polyglandular disease.
## Epidemiology
No quantified direct association.
## Pathophysiology
Severe hypothyroidism or associated adrenal deficit may alter metabolism.
## Clinical presentation
Thyroid symptoms and fasting hypoglycemia if polyglandular.
## Diagnostic criteria
Simultaneous low glucose and complete endocrine review.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| TSH and thyroxine | Assess thyroid |
| Cortisol and ACTH | Exclude Addison disease |
| Pituitary hormones | Exclude hypopituitarism |

## Differential diagnosis
Addison disease, hypopituitarism and medicines.
## Management
Treat confirmed deficit.
## Complications and prognosis
Adrenal crisis or hypoglycemia if coexisting.
## Follow-up
Monitor thyroid and adrenal axes.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
