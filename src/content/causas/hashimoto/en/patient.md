## What it is
Hashimoto is an autoimmune thyroid disease.
## Quick summary
- It may coexist with Addison or hypopituitarism.
- It does not explain hypoglycemia alone.
- Glucose must be measured during symptoms.
- Thyroid function requires review.
- Associated deficits should be treated.
- Direct evidence is limited.
## Symptoms
Fatigue, cold sensitivity, weight change, weakness or confusion.
## Why it happens
Severe hypothyroidism or polyglandular disease may coexist. The exact mechanism needs confirmation.
## Who may have it
People with several autoimmune diseases or family history.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose, TSH, thyroxine, cortisol and other hormones as indicated.
## Treatment
Treat confirmed thyroid and hormonal deficits.
## Food and eating
Regular meals without unnecessary restriction.
## What to do during an episode
If conscious and glucose is low, take rapid glucose.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for persistent confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which hormone deficit accompanies thyroid disease?
- Is hypoglycemia confirmed?
- Are Addison or hypopituitarism present?
## More information
Specialized endocrine care.
