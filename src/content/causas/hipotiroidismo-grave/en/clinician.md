## Clinical summary
Variable hypoglycemia, frequent with associated adrenal or pituitary deficit.
## Definition
Profound hypothyroidism with systemic effects.
## Epidemiology
Isolated association is limited.
## Pathophysiology
Low metabolism may reduce gluconeogenesis and glucose availability.
## Clinical presentation
Hypothermia, bradycardia, somnolence and hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Simultaneous low glucose and low thyroid function.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| TSH and thyroxine | Confirms hypothyroidism |
| Cortisol and ACTH | Excludes associated deficit |
| Electrolytes | Assess hyponatremia |

## Differential diagnosis
Hypopituitarism, Addison disease and fasting.
## Management
Thyroid replacement and associated deficits.
## Complications and prognosis
Myxedema coma and death.
## Follow-up
Hormone titration and glucose.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
