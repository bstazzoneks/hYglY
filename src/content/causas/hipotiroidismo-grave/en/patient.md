<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: fasting.</strong> Hypoglycemia often requires another associated cause.</aside>
## What it is
The thyroid makes too little thyroid hormone.
## Quick summary
- It may accompany hypopituitarism or Addison disease.
- It does not strongly explain isolated hypoglycemia.
- Glucose and hormones require measurement.
- Hyponatremia and cold sensitivity may occur.
- Replacement requires titration.
## Symptoms
Fatigue, cold sensitivity, confusion and weakness.
## Why it happens
Low metabolism may reduce glucose availability. Adrenal insufficiency may coexist.
## Who may have it
People with thyroiditis, surgery, hypopituitarism or polyglandular disease.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and review TSH, thyroxine, cortisol and ACTH.
## Treatment
Supervised thyroid replacement. Treat associated causes.
## Food and eating
Regular meals without prolonged fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if conscious and recheck.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for extreme sleepiness, hypothermia or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Do I have another hormone deficiency?
- Is low glucose confirmed?
- Which replacement is safe?
