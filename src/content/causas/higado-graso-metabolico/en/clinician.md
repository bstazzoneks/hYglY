## Clinical summary
Review advanced liver disease before attributing hypoglycemia.
## Definition
Fat accumulation associated with metabolic factors.
## Epidemiology
Hypoglycemia is not generalized in early disease.
## Pathophysiology
Resistance and, in advanced damage, reduced gluconeogenesis and glycogenolysis.
## Clinical presentation
Variable fasting or postprandial symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Simultaneous low glucose and liver assessment.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Liver tests | Assess damage |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Separate mechanism |

## Differential diagnosis
Cirrhosis, alcohol, medicines, kidney failure and tumors.
## Management
Metabolic and hepatologic control.
## Complications and prognosis
Progressive liver damage and neuroglycopenic episodes.
## Follow-up
Monitor liver function and recurrence.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and advanced cirrhosis.
