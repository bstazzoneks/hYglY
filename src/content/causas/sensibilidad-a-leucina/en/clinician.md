## Clinical summary
Endogenous hypoglycemia after protein stimulation.
## Definition
Hyperinsulinism phenotype triggered by leucine.
## Epidemiology
Rare.
## Pathophysiology
Excess pancreatic secretion after amino acids.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial symptoms after protein.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose, insulin, C-peptide and drug exclusion.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Controlled test | Specialist only |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, NIPHS and sulfonylureas.
## Management
Specialized diet and medicines by team.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Metabolic monitoring.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and pregnancy.
