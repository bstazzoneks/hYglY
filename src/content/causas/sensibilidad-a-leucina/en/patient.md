<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: postprandial.</strong> It may relate to leucine-rich protein meals.</aside>
## What it is
Leucine may trigger excessive insulin release.
## Quick summary
- It is rare.
- Protein-rich meals may trigger it.
- Critical testing is required.
- Insulinoma and medicines must be excluded.
- Nutrition care is specialized.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, confusion or seizures.
## Why it happens
Insulin secretion may exceed glucose availability.
## Who may have it
Children or adults with familial hyperinsulinism.
## How it is diagnosed
Protein history, critical sample and genetic testing as indicated.
## Treatment
Specialized diet and medicines only with the care team.
## Food and eating
Do not restrict protein alone.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able and recheck.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness or seizures.
## Daily life
Carry a food plan and avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Does protein trigger episodes?
- Do I need genetic testing?
- Which nutrition plan is safe?
