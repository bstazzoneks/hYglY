## What it is
The enzyme cannot convert certain precursors into glucose.
## Quick summary
- Fasting may trigger hypoglycemia.
- Illness or vomiting increases risk.
- Acidosis may occur.
- Genetics and metabolic testing confirm it.
- Avoid fasting without supervision.
- Pediatric plans are essential.
## Symptoms
Weakness, fast breathing, confusion, sleepiness or seizures.
## Why it happens
Gluconeogenesis is impaired and glucose is not maintained.
## Who may have it
Children or adults with known metabolic diagnosis.
## How it is diagnosed
History, glucose, lactate and specialized genetic testing.
## Treatment
Avoid fasting and treat infections. Use rapid glucose under supervision.
## Food and eating
Frequent meals and metabolic plan. Do not use prolonged fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able to swallow and recheck after 15 minutes.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for vomiting, fast breathing, seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Plan travel and illness with the metabolic team.
