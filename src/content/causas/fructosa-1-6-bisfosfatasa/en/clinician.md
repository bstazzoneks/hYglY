## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia with elevated lactate during fasting or illness.
## Definition
Fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase deficiency.
## Epidemiology
Rare.
## Pathophysiology
Gluconeogenesis defect with acidosis.
## Clinical presentation
Vomiting, fast breathing, hypoglycemia and seizures.
## Diagnostic criteria
Glucose, lactate, acid-base and genetics.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Lactate and pH | Assess acidosis |
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Genetics | Confirms |
## Differential diagnosis
Glycogen storage disease and fatty acid oxidation defects.
## Management
Avoid fasting and treat infection.
## Complications and prognosis
Severe acidosis and neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Metabolic and nutrition care.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and acute illness.
