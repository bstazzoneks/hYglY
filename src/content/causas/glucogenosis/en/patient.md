<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: fasting.</strong> Pattern varies by glycogen storage disease type.</aside>
## What it is
Inherited errors affect liver or muscle glycogen.
## Quick summary
- Fasting may trigger hypoglycemia.
- Liver enlargement or muscle fatigue may occur.
- Type determines management.
- Metabolic care is required.
- Avoid long fasts.
- Exercise requires supervision.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, fatigue or seizures during fasting.
## Why it happens
Glucose cannot be released from glycogen or used normally.
## Who may have it
Children or adults with prior metabolic diagnosis.
## How it is diagnosed
History, glucose, lactate, lipids and specialized genetics.
## Treatment
Frequent feeding, cornstarch or other measures according to type.
## Food and eating
Follow the metabolic plan. Do not change timing alone.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able to swallow and recheck after 15 minutes.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness, seizures or prolonged illness.
## Daily life
Plan exercise, school and travel with the team.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which type do I have?
- What fasting interval is safe?
- Which plan applies during illness?
## More information
Pediatric or adult metabolic specialists.
