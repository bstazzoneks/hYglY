## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia varies by liver or muscle glycogen disorder.
## Definition
Inherited synthesis or degradation defects.
## Epidemiology
Rare. Liver and muscle types differ.
## Pathophysiology
Glucose cannot be maintained during fasting or exercise.
## Clinical presentation
Hypoglycemia, hepatomegaly, lactate or muscle weakness.
## Diagnostic criteria
Glucose, lactate, lipids, enzymes and genetics.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Supervised fast | Characterizes |
| Lactate and lipids | Separate types |
| Genetics | Confirms |
## Differential diagnosis
Fructose disorders, fatty oxidation defects and cortisol deficit.
## Management
Frequent feeding and type-specific measures.
## Complications and prognosis
Liver, kidney or muscle disease.
## Follow-up
Metabolic and nutrition care.
## Special populations
Pediatric care, pregnancy and exercise.
