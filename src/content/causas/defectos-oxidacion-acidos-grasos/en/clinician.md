## Clinical summary
Hypoketotic hypoglycemia with fasting or illness.
## Definition
Mitochondrial beta-oxidation defects.
## Epidemiology
Varies by disorder and region.
## Pathophysiology
Fat oxidation, ketogenesis and gluconeogenesis fail.
## Clinical presentation
Vomiting, lethargy, hypoglycemia and myopathy.
## Diagnostic criteria
Glucose, low ketones, acylcarnitines and genetics.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Acylcarnitines | Metabolic profile |
| Urine organic acids | Supports diagnosis |
| Genetics | Confirms type |
## Differential diagnosis
Glycogen storage disease, fasting and endocrine disease.
## Management
Avoid fasting, treat illness and provide specific follow-up.
## Complications and prognosis
Myopathy, cardiac injury and sudden death during crisis.
## Follow-up
Metabolic care and cardiology according to defect.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and pregnancy.
