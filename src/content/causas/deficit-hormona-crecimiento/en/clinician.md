## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia is more relevant in children and multiple pituitary deficits.
## Definition
Deficit confirmed with stimulated testing.
## Epidemiology
Rare as an isolated adult cause.
## Pathophysiology
Reduced metabolic reserve and possible concomitant ACTH deficit.
## Clinical presentation
Fasting hypoglycemia or neuroglycopenic symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low sample and specialist stimulated evaluation.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Stimulated test | Confirms deficit |
| IGF-1 | Supports evaluation |
| Cortisol and ACTH | Exclude associated deficit |

## Differential diagnosis
Hypopituitarism, Addison disease and endogenous hypoglycemia.
## Management
Supervised replacement.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury and growth impairment.
## Follow-up
Growth, glucose and hormone replacement.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and pregnancy.
