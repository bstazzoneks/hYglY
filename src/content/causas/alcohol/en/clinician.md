## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from reduced hepatic glucose production in alcohol exposure or fasting.
## Definition
Alcohol-related blockade of hepatic glucose production.
## Epidemiology
More frequent with malnutrition and fasting.
## Pathophysiology
Alcohol metabolites reduce NAD availability.
## Clinical presentation
Confusion, somnolence and low glucose.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and exclusion of insulin or medicines.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Alcohol | Provides context |

## Differential diagnosis
Intoxication alone, insulin and sepsis.
## Management
Glucose and nutrition; prevent recurrence.
## Complications and prognosis
Cerebral injury and recurrence.
## Follow-up
Abstinence and nutritional support.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and diabetes.
