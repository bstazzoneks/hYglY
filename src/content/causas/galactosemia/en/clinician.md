## Clinical summary
Metabolic hypoglycemia from galactose exposure and liver injury.
## Definition
Inherited galactose pathway disorder.
## Epidemiology
Detected by neonatal screening.
## Pathophysiology
Accumulation alters hepatic glucose and other organs.
## Clinical presentation
Vomiting, liver injury, jaundice and hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Screening, metabolites and genetics.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

## Differential diagnosis
Hereditary fructose intolerance and glycogen storage disease.
## Management
Metabolic diet and acute care.
## Complications and prognosis
Liver, kidney or neurologic injury.
## Follow-up
Metabolic and nutrition care.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and pregnancy.
