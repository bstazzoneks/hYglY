## Clinical summary
Endogenous hypoglycemia mediated by antibodies with out-of-sync free insulin.
## Definition
High-capacity antibodies bind insulin and release it later.
## Epidemiology
Rare. Regional and drug associations have been reported.
## Pathophysiology
Antibody-insulin complexes release insulin irregularly.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial or spontaneous hypoglycemia, sometimes after hyperglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose, elevated C-peptide and endogenous insulin, positive antibodies and excluded exposure.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Anti-insulin antibodies | Confirms mechanism |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Distinguishes endogenous origin |
| Sulfonylurea screen | Excludes drug exposure |

## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, NIPHS, sulfonylureas and factitious hypoglycemia.
## Management
Remove a confirmed trigger. Severe persistent cases may require corticosteroids or immunosuppression.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic events and variable remission.
## Follow-up
Monitor antibodies, glucose and medicines.
## Special populations
Pregnancy, pediatric patients and older adults require adaptation.
