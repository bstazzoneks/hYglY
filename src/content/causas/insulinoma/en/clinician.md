## Clinical summary
Endogenous hypoglycemia from pancreatic beta-cell tumor.
## Definition
Insulin-secreting neoplasm.
## Epidemiology
Rare.
## Pathophysiology
Autonomous insulin secretion.
## Clinical presentation
Neuroglycopenic fasting hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple, elevated insulin and C-peptide, ketone suppression and drug exclusion.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed. Follow supervised fasting protocols.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Supervised fast | Confirms endogenous hypoglycemia |
| Endoscopic ultrasound or MRI | Localizes tumor |
| Selective sampling | Complex cases |
## Differential diagnosis
NIPHS, medicines, factitious hypoglycemia and IGF tumors.
## Management
Surgical resection; temporary medical measures.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury and recurrence.
## Follow-up
Glucose and postoperative control.
## Special populations
MEN1 and pediatric care.
