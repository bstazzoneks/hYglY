## What it is
Several medicines have rare or reversible associations.
## Quick summary
- Reported medicines include fluoroquinolones, beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, salicylates and disopyramide.
- Recombinant IGF-1 may also contribute.
- Do not stop treatments alone.
- Glucose should be measured during symptoms.
- The team reviews exposure and kidney risk.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, confusion and loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Mechanisms vary by medicine and metabolic state.
## Who may have it
People exposed to these medicines, especially with kidney failure or fasting.
## How it is diagnosed
Medicine review, critical glucose and insulin/C-peptide as appropriate.
## Treatment
Supervised withdrawal or adjustment and acute treatment.
## Food and eating
Regular meals according to plan.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which medicine could cause it?
- Was exposure recent?
- Which alternative is safe?
