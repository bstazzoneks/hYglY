## Clinical summary
Rare or context-dependent drug hypoglycemia.
## Definition
Associations not included in main categories.
## Epidemiology
Depends on exposure and comorbidity.
## Pathophysiology
Varies: insulin, IGF signal, hepatic or renal toxicity.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial, fasting or intercurrent.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose, timing and exclusion of endogenous disease.

## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, sulfonylureas and alcohol.
## Management
Supervised withdrawal or adjustment.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia or recurrence.
## Follow-up
Pharmacologic reassessment.
## Special populations
Recombinant IGF-1, pregnancy and pediatric care.
