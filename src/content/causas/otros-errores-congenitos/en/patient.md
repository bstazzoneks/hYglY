## What it is
Many rare disorders alter glucose, fat or amino acid metabolism.
## Quick summary
- Each disorder has a different pattern.
- Fasting and infection are common triggers.
- Specialized laboratories are required.
- Unsupported associations are omitted.
- Refer to metabolic care.
## Symptoms
Hypoglycemia, vomiting, sleepiness, seizures or weakness.
## Why it happens
The altered pathway varies by disease.
## Who may have it
Children or adults with familial metabolic history.
## How it is diagnosed
Critical sample, acylcarnitines, organic acids and genetics.
## Treatment
Disease-specific plan.
## Food and eating
Do not change diet without specialist guidance.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able and recheck.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures, confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Carry an illness plan and medical history.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which metabolic pathway is affected?
- Which test confirms the defect?
- Which plan applies during illness?
