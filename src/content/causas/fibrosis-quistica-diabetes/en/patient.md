<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: both.</strong> It may occur with treatment or fasting.</aside>
## What it is
Cystic fibrosis can damage pancreatic insulin secretion and cause diabetes.
## Quick summary
- Insulin deficiency and late mismatches may occur.
- Symptoms require measurement.
- Medicines and nutrition influence glucose.
- Infections may change glucose.
- The plan needs respiratory and endocrine expertise.
- Do not adjust insulin alone.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, hunger, confusion or fatigue.
## Why it happens
Pancreatic fibrosis reduces insulin. Malabsorption and treatment may create variability.
## Who may have it
People with cystic fibrosis and diagnosed diabetes.
## How it is diagnosed
Document low glucose with symptoms. Review insulin, nutrition, infection and pancreatic function.
## Treatment
Individual endocrine-respiratory plan. Adjust insulin only under supervision.
## Food and eating
Maintain the specialized nutrition plan. Avoid fasting or restriction without direction.
## What to do during an episode
If conscious and glucose is low, take rapid glucose and recheck after 15 minutes.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness, seizures or inability to eat.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms. Plan sport and infections with the team.
## Questions for your doctor
- Is hypoglycemia from insulin or malabsorption?
- Did infection change glucose?
- Do I need insulin or food adjustment?
## More information
Specialized cystic fibrosis and diabetes teams.
