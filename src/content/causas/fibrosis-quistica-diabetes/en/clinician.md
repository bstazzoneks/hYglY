## Clinical summary
Distinguish treatment mismatch from endogenous insulin loss.
## Definition
Hypoglycemia in cystic fibrosis-related diabetes.
## Epidemiology
Frequency varies by age and severity.
## Pathophysiology
Pancreatic dysfunction, malabsorption and infection.
## Clinical presentation
Alternating hyperglycemia, postprandial or fasting hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical sample and Whipple's triad.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses reserve |
| Nutrition and infection | Provide context |

## Differential diagnosis
Insulin mismatch, malabsorption, alcohol and fasting.
## Management
Endocrine-respiratory team and individual adjustment.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury and malnutrition.
## Follow-up
Specialist monitoring and education.
## Special populations
Pediatric care, pregnancy and transplantation.
