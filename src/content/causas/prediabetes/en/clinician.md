<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Insulin resistance may precede hyperinsulinism. Postprandial symptoms do not confirm hypoglycemia.
## Definition
Prediabetes uses standard glucose criteria. Hypoglycemia requires Whipple's triad.
## Epidemiology
Postprandial hypoglycemia prevalence is not established.
## Pathophysiology
Compensatory hyperinsulinism and changing tissue sensitivity.
## Clinical presentation
Symptoms after meals with variable glucose.
## Diagnostic criteria
Document low glucose with symptoms and improvement.
## Tests and cutoff values
No dedicated hypoglycemia cutoff is listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Serum glucose | Confirms episode |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses hyperinsulinism |
| HbA1c and oral glucose test | Assesses prediabetes |

## Differential diagnosis
Anxiety, dumping, medicines, insulinoma and physiologic decline.
## Management
Activity, regular food and metabolic control.
## Complications and prognosis
Risk is missed diagnosis or delayed diabetes recognition.
## Follow-up
Monitor metabolic progression and recurrence.
## Special populations
Adapt for pregnancy, pediatric patients and older adults.
