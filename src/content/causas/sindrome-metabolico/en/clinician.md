<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Confirm hypoglycemia before attributing symptoms to the syndrome.
## Definition
Metabolic syndrome combines central adiposity, blood pressure, lipid and glucose criteria.
## Epidemiology
Associated true hypoglycemia is not quantified.
## Pathophysiology
Insulin resistance and compensatory hyperinsulinism.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial symptoms or nonspecific fatigue.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple's triad and metabolic criteria are separate.
## Tests and cutoff values
No hypoglycemia values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses hyperinsulinism |
| Metabolic profile | Characterizes syndrome |

## Differential diagnosis
Anxiety, dumping, medicines and early diabetes.
## Management
Activity, food and cardiovascular control.
## Complications and prognosis
Food restriction or delayed alternative diagnosis.
## Follow-up
Monitor glucose and metabolic factors.
## Special populations
Adapt for pregnancy and older adults.
