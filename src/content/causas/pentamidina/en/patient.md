<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: both.</strong> It may alternate early hypoglycemia and later hyperglycemia.</aside>
## What it is
Pentamidine may release insulin and injure beta cells.
## Quick summary
- Early hypoglycemia may occur.
- Hyperglycemia may follow.
- Glucose should be monitored.
- Do not stop medicine alone.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, confusion or weakness.
## Why it happens
Pancreatic injury alters insulin.
## Who may have it
People receiving pentamidine.
## How it is diagnosed
Serial glucose and insulin/C-peptide review.
## Treatment
Monitoring and team-directed adjustment.
## Food and eating
Regular meals and rapid glucose.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Do I need frequent glucose checks?
- Can hyperglycemia occur later?
- Which plan applies during treatment?
## More information
Infectious disease and endocrine teams.
