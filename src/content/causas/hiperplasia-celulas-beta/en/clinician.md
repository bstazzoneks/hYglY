## Clinical summary
Endogenous hypoglycemia without demonstrated focal tumor.
## Definition
Hyperplasia or abnormal beta-cell distribution.
## Epidemiology
Rare and overlapping with NIPHS.
## Pathophysiology
Increased insulin secretion.
## Clinical presentation
Fasting or postprandial pattern.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple and critical sample; special localization if imaging is negative.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Insulin and C-peptide | Confirm origin |
| Selective sampling | Localize |
| Histology | Confirm pattern |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, NIPHS and medicines.
## Management
Diet, medicines or surgery according to pattern.
## Complications and prognosis
Persistent hypoglycemia.
## Follow-up
Endocrine monitoring.
## Special populations
Post-bariatric and pediatric cases.
