## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from restriction or purging.
## Definition
Malnutrition in an eating disorder.
## Epidemiology
Depends on severity.
## Pathophysiology
Reduced reserves and gluconeogenesis.
## Clinical presentation
Weakness, syncope and hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and multidisciplinary review.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Electrolytes | Assess risk |
| Liver/kidney | Assess reserve |
## Differential diagnosis
Malnutrition, endocrine and medicine causes.
## Management
Gradual nutrition and psychological support.
## Complications and prognosis
Refeeding syndrome and cerebral injury.
## Follow-up
Specialist team.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
