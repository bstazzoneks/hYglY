## What it is
Restriction, purging or malnutrition may exhaust reserves.
## Quick summary
- True hypoglycemia may occur.
- Medical specialist care is required.
- Avoid self-fasting or purging.
- Measure glucose and treat dehydration.
- Seek mental health support without blame.
## Symptoms
Weakness, dizziness, confusion and loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Low nutrients reduce glycogen and liver production.
## Who may have it
People with restriction, binge-purge behavior or malnutrition.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and assess nutrition, electrolytes and hormones.
## Treatment
Gradual nutrition plan and psychological support.
## Food and eating
Only with a specialist team.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose and take rapid glucose if low.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for confusion, seizures or severe dehydration.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms and seek support.
## Questions for your doctor
- Is hypoglycemia confirmed?
- Which nutrition plan is safe?
- Which mental health support is needed?
## More information
Specialized eating disorder teams.
