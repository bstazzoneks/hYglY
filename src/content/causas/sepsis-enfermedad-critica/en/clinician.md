## Clinical summary
Critical hypoglycemia with underlying high mortality.
## Definition
Hypoglycemia associated with sepsis or organ dysfunction.
## Epidemiology
Associated with severe disease and malnutrition.
## Pathophysiology
Increased consumption and reduced production.
## Clinical presentation
Confusion, hypothermia or critical illness.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and infection evaluation.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Lactate | Assess severity |
| Cultures | Confirm infection |
| Liver function | Assess reserve |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulin, alcohol and critical care medicine.
## Management
Hospital support and cause treatment.
## Complications and prognosis
Neurologic injury and death.
## Follow-up
Intensive care.
## Special populations
Pediatric and older adult care.
