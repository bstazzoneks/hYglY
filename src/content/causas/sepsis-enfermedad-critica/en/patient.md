## What it is
Severe infection may alter glucose through stress, fasting and liver dysfunction.
## Quick summary
- It may be a medical emergency.
- It may mimic infection symptoms.
- Urgent glucose measurement is needed.
- Hospital supervision is required.
## Symptoms
Confusion, weakness, fever, rapid breathing and loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
The body consumes glucose and fails to produce or release it.
## Who may have it
People with sepsis, malnutrition, insulin exposure or liver disease.
## How it is diagnosed
Critical glucose, lactate, infection and organ function testing.
## Treatment
Hospital care, glucose correction and treatment of the cause.
## Food and eating
Follow hospital team guidance.
## What to do during an episode
Call emergency services for severe confusion or loss of consciousness.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for fever with confusion, seizures or persistent low glucose.
## Daily life
Do not delay assessment if infection is suspected.
## Questions for your doctor
- What caused hypoglycemia?
- Is there sepsis or liver dysfunction?
- Which follow-up is needed?
