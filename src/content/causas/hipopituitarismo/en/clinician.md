## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from pituitary hormone deficits, especially ACTH.
## Definition
Partial or complete pituitary axis deficits.
## Epidemiology
Uncommon, especially recognized after surgery or in children.
## Pathophysiology
Cortisol and growth hormone deficits reduce hepatic glucose.
## Clinical presentation
Weakness, confusion, headache or visual change.
## Diagnostic criteria
Simultaneous low glucose and hormone testing.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Cortisol and ACTH | Assess adrenal axis |
| TSH and thyroxine | Assess thyroid axis |
| Pituitary MRI | Assess structural cause |

## Differential diagnosis
Primary Addison disease, prolonged fasting and medicines.
## Management
Specific replacement and stress adjustment.
## Complications and prognosis
Crisis, visual change and neuroglycopenia.
## Follow-up
Monitor each axis and imaging.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
