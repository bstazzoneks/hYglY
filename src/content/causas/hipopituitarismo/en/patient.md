## What it is
The pituitary makes too little hormone for other glands.
## Quick summary
- Low ACTH lowers cortisol.
- Low growth hormone may affect glucose.
- Headache or visual change may occur.
- Tumor, surgery or immunotherapy may cause it.
- Hormone studies are needed.
- Replacement requires follow-up.
## Symptoms
Weakness, nausea, cold sensitivity, confusion and dizziness.
## Why it happens
Hormone deficits reduce liver glucose output and counter-regulation.
## Who may have it
People with pituitary surgery, radiation, tumor, bleeding or immunotherapy.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and review cortisol, ACTH, TSH and pituitary hormones.
## Treatment
Supervised hormone replacement.
## Food and eating
Regular meals without prolonged fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose and take rapid glucose if low.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness, seizures or severe headache.
## Daily life
Carry a medical card and avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which hormone axis is low?
- Do I have ACTH deficiency?
- What plan applies during illness?
