## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from known or hidden exposure.
## Definition
Exogenous insulin or secretagogue without endogenous disease.
## Epidemiology
Depends on access and context.
## Pathophysiology
Endogenous suppression or drug stimulation.
## Clinical presentation
Recurrent episodes.
## Diagnostic criteria
Insulin, C-peptide, antibodies and drug screen.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| C-peptide | Differentiates |
| Drug screen | Confirms secretagogue |
| Insulin | Assesses exogenous exposure |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma and NIPHS.
## Management
Safety and psychological support.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Multidisciplinary team.
## Special populations
Pediatric and pregnant patients.
