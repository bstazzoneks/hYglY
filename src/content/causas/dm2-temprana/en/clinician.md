<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Review physiologic and pharmacologic hyperinsulinism.
## Definition
Documented episode after recognition of early diabetes.
## Epidemiology
Poorly defined without medicines.
## Pathophysiology
Delayed or prolonged insulin response.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial or post-exercise symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple's triad with a simultaneous sample.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses origin |
| Drug screening | Excludes exposure |

## Differential diagnosis
Medicines, dumping, insulinoma and gastroparesis.
## Management
Adjust cause and diabetes treatment.
## Complications and prognosis
Falls, neuroglycopenia and overtreatment.
## Follow-up
Monitor, review food and medicines.
## Special populations
Pregnancy, pediatric patients and older adults.
