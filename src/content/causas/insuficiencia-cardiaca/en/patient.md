## What it is
Advanced heart disease may alter nutrition and metabolism.
## Quick summary
- Symptoms may mimic decompensation.
- Medicines and liver disease may contribute.
- Glucose should be measured.
- Emergency care applies for confusion or decompensation.
## Symptoms
Weakness, confusion, sweating and dizziness.
## Why it happens
Limited reserves and organ dysfunction may lower glucose.
## Who may have it
People with advanced heart failure, malnutrition or treated diabetes.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and review liver, kidney and medicines.
## Treatment
Treat heart disease and hypoglycemia cause.
## Food and eating
Follow heart plan and avoid fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose and take rapid glucose if able.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for breathlessness, confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Is there malnutrition or liver dysfunction?
- Which medicine may prolong low glucose?
- Do I need monitoring?
## More information
Cardiology and endocrine teams.
