## Clinical summary
Secondary hypoglycemia in advanced heart disease.
## Definition
Contextual association, not isolated primary cause.
## Epidemiology
Evidence is limited.
## Pathophysiology
Organ dysfunction, malnutrition and medicines.
## Clinical presentation
Weakness, confusion and decompensation.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and secondary assessment.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Liver/kidney function | Assess reserve |
| Drug review | Excludes medicine |
## Differential diagnosis
Malnutrition, alcohol and medicines.
## Management
Treat primary cause.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Cardiology and nutrition.
## Special populations
Older adults.
