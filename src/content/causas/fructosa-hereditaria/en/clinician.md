## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia after fructose, sucrose or sorbitol exposure.
## Definition
Inherited aldolase B deficiency.
## Epidemiology
Rare. Timing depends on exposure.
## Pathophysiology
Phosphate ester accumulation inhibits hepatic glucose production.
## Clinical presentation
Vomiting, hypoglycemia, liver injury and aversion to sweet food.
## Diagnostic criteria
History, genetics and metabolic testing. Avoid unsupervised oral challenge.

## Differential diagnosis
Galactosemia, glycogen storage disease and fasting.
## Management
Avoid fructose, sucrose and sorbitol.
## Complications and prognosis
Liver or kidney injury with persistent exposure.
## Follow-up
Metabolic, nutrition and growth review.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
