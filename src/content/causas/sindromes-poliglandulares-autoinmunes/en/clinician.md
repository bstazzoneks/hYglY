## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia associated with multiple counter-regulatory hormone deficiencies.
## Definition
Syndromes grouping autoimmune gland diseases.
## Epidemiology
Uncommon, with type 1 and type 2 patterns.
## Pathophysiology
Cortisol and other hormone deficits reduce low-glucose responses.
## Clinical presentation
Weakness, nausea, hyponatremia, hypocalcemia or hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Document low glucose and simultaneous hormone deficiency.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Cortisol and ACTH | Assess Addison disease |
| TSH and thyroid antibodies | Assess thyroiditis |
| Calcium and PTH | Assess hypoparathyroidism |

## Differential diagnosis
Hypopituitarism, isolated Addison disease and medicines.
## Management
Hormone replacement and crisis education.
## Complications and prognosis
Adrenal crisis and neuroglycopenic episodes.
## Follow-up
Monitor all associated glands.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
