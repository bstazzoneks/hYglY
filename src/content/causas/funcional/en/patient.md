<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong> Low glucose is not confirmed in many people with this category.</aside>

## What it is
Functional hypoglycemia describes compatible symptoms without demonstrated low glucose. Whipple's triad should be fulfilled to confirm true hypoglycemia.

## Quick summary
- Symptoms may resemble hypoglycemia.
- Glucose should be measured during an episode.
- Other problems can produce the same symptoms.
- A rapid fall may cause symptoms without low glucose.
- Diagnosis requires clinical evaluation.
- Home readings do not replace laboratory samples.

## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, hunger, fatigue or trouble concentrating. Dizziness or anxiety may occur.

## Why it happens
A rapid decline from a postprandial peak may cause symptoms. No single mechanism is confirmed.

## Who may have it
Adults with postprandial symptoms may be assessed. Surgery, medicines, early diabetes and hormone disorders should be excluded.

## How it is diagnosed
A clinician measures symptoms, glucose and improvement after correction. Supervised measurements may be requested.

## Treatment
Treatment begins by confirming or excluding hypoglycemia. Alternative factors are reviewed without automatic restrictive diets.

## Food and eating
Keep regular meals and reasonable portions. Do not fast or eliminate food groups without guidance.

## What to do during an episode
If conscious, measure glucose. If low, take rapid glucose, wait 15 minutes and recheck.

Call emergency services for loss of consciousness or seizures.

## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for persistent confusion, seizures or inability to recover.

## Daily life
Do not drive during symptoms. Keep a record of timing and measurements.

## Questions for your doctor
- Was low glucose confirmed?
- Was Whipple's triad complete?
- Which alternative diagnoses should be excluded?
- Which supervised test is useful?
- Which changes are safe?

## More information
Ask a primary care or endocrine clinician for review.
