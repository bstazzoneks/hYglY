## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from prolonged drug exposure.
## Definition
Secretagogue inducing insulin independently of glucose.
## Epidemiology
High risk in older adults and kidney disease.
## Pathophysiology
Endogenous insulin rises through K_ATP channel blockade.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial or fasting, prolonged recurrence.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose, elevated insulin and C-peptide, positive drug screen.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Sulfonylurea screen | Confirms exposure |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Confirms endogenous origin |
| Kidney function | Predicts duration |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, factitious, NIPHS and alcohol.
## Management
Stop agent and provide prolonged observation according to severity.
## Complications and prognosis
Recurrence and neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Adjust diabetes and review older adults.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
