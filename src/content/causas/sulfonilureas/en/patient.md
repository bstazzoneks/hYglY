<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: both.</strong> It may occur after meals or during prolonged fasting.</aside>
## What it is
Sulfonylureas stimulate insulin regardless of glucose level.
## Quick summary
- Episodes may last many hours.
- Missed meals, alcohol or illness increase risk.
- Glucose should be measured during symptoms.
- The active generic name should be reviewed.
- Do not change doses alone.
- Seek urgent care if it does not improve.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, hunger, confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Medicine stimulates beta cells and may remain active.
## Who may have it
People with diabetes using sulfonylureas, especially older adults or kidney disease.
## How it is diagnosed
Critical glucose plus insulin, C-peptide and drug screening.
## Treatment
The team adjusts or changes medicine. Severe episodes may need prolonged observation.
## Food and eating
Keep the advised meal schedule and avoid alcohol without guidance.
## What to do during an episode
If able to swallow, take rapid glucose and recheck after 15 minutes. Repeat once.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness, seizures or inability to eat.
## Daily life
Do not drive during symptoms and carry rapid glucose.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which sulfonylurea do I take?
- Does age or kidney function require adjustment?
- Which plan applies if I miss a meal?
