## Clinical summary
Autoimmune receptor dysfunction alternates hyperglycemic and hypoglycemic phases.
## Definition
Antibodies block or stimulate insulin receptor signaling.
## Epidemiology
Rare and associated with lupus, Sjogren disease, scleroderma or other autoimmune disease.
## Pathophysiology
Variable receptor modulation causes resistance or excess effect.
## Clinical presentation
Insulin-resistant hyperglycemia followed sometimes by hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Demonstrate antibodies and exclude exogenous insulin, tumors and medicines.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Anti-receptor antibodies | Confirms mechanism |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses endogenous source |
| Autoimmune testing | Documents association |

## Differential diagnosis
Hirata syndrome, insulinoma, factitious hypoglycemia and uncontrolled diabetes.
## Management
Specialized immunomodulation and alternating glucose management.
## Complications and prognosis
Dehydration, ketosis, neuroglycopenia and variable remission.
## Follow-up
Monitor antibodies, glucose and systemic disease.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care require specialized teams.
