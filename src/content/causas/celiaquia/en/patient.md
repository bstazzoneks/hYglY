## What it is
Celiac disease causes immune intestinal injury after gluten exposure.
## Quick summary
- It may coexist with Addison disease or type 1 diabetes.
- Gluten-free food alone does not explain hypoglycemia.
- Glucose and hormones require measurement.
- Malabsorption or malnutrition may occur.
- Symptoms do not prove hypoglycemia.
- Direct evidence is limited.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, fatigue or confusion.
## Why it happens
Malnutrition, adrenal insufficiency or associated diabetes may occur.
## Who may have it
People with celiac disease and another autoimmune disorder.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and review hormones, nutrition and malabsorption.
## Treatment
Treat the confirmed cause and maintain a gluten-free diet when appropriate.
## Food and eating
Do not change diet without confirmation. Seek specialist nutrition support.
## What to do during an episode
If conscious and glucose is low, take rapid glucose.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for persistent confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Is there another endocrine cause?
- Is there malnutrition or malabsorption?
- What test confirmed hypoglycemia?
## More information
