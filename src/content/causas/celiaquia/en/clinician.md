## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia should be attributed to a confirmed associated cause.
## Definition
Gluten-associated autoimmune intestinal disease.
## Epidemiology
Direct hypoglycemia association is not quantified.
## Pathophysiology
Malabsorption or polyglandular disease may contribute.
## Clinical presentation
Gastrointestinal symptoms and variable glucose.
## Diagnostic criteria
Document low glucose and complete endocrine review.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms episode |
| Nutrition and albumin | Assesses malnutrition |
| Adrenal hormones | Exclude Addison disease |

## Differential diagnosis
Addison disease, treated diabetes and malabsorption.
## Management
Treat confirmed cause and nutrition.
## Complications and prognosis
Malnutrition and neuroglycopenic episodes.
## Follow-up
Monitor diet and endocrine associations.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
