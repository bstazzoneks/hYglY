## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from limited reserve or altered medicine clearance.
## Definition
Advanced liver or kidney disease.
## Epidemiology
More frequent in advanced cirrhosis, kidney failure and malnutrition.
## Pathophysiology
Gluconeogenesis and clearance are altered.
## Clinical presentation
Intermittent or persistent hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and organ assessment.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Liver function | Assess reserve |
| Kidney function | Assess clearance |
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
## Differential diagnosis
Medicines, alcohol and insulinoma.
## Management
Treat organ disease and adjust doses.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and advanced disease.
## Follow-up
Hepatic, renal and nutrition care.
## Special populations
Diabetes and older adults.
