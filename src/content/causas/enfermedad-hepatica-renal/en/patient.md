<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: fasting.</strong> It may occur with advanced disease, malnutrition or medicines.</aside>
## What it is
Liver and kidneys affect glucose production and medicine removal.
## Quick summary
- Hypoglycemia may indicate advanced disease.
- Medicines may remain active longer.
- Glucose should be measured during symptoms.
- Insulin and alcohol must be excluded.
- Nutrition needs supervision.
## Symptoms
Weakness, confusion, trembling or loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Lower liver production or altered elimination may lower glucose.
## Who may have it
People with cirrhosis, kidney failure, malnutrition or multiple medicines.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and review liver, kidney and medicine exposure.
## Treatment
Treat organ disease and adjust medicines. Severe hypoglycemia needs urgent care.
## Food and eating
Regular meals according to liver or kidney plan. Avoid fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able and recheck after 15 minutes.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness, seizures or persistent confusion.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms and report medicine changes.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which liver or kidney stage do I have?
- Which medicines may remain active?
- Do I need a specific nutrition plan?
## More information
Liver, kidney or endocrine teams.
