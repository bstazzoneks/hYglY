## What it is
Some autoimmune diseases share endocrine mechanisms with hypoglycemia.
## Quick summary
- Only citable associations are included.
- Hypoglycemia must be confirmed.
- Adrenal and pituitary disease have stronger relevance.
- Associations without source are omitted.
- Treatment depends on the confirmed disease.
## Symptoms
Low glucose or hormone deficiency symptoms.
## Why it happens
Hormone counter-regulation or antibodies may contribute.
## Who may have it
People with several autoimmune diseases.
## How it is diagnosed
Apply Whipple's triad and review hormones, antibodies and medicines.
## Treatment
Individualized by confirmed cause.
## Food and eating
Regular meals without unnecessary restriction.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose and treat a low value.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for loss of consciousness or seizures.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which association has evidence?
- Is hormone deficiency present?
- Which test confirms mechanism?
## More information
Specialized endocrine care.
