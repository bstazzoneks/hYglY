<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia linked to administration without food.
## Definition
Short-acting secretagogue.
## Epidemiology
Shorter duration than sulfonylureas but risk remains.
## Pathophysiology
Temporary pancreatic stimulation.
## Clinical presentation
Symptoms near skipped meals.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and timing correlation.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assess origin |

## Differential diagnosis
Sulfonylureas, insulin and insulinoma.
## Management
Adjust timing or medicine.
## Complications and prognosis
Recurrent episodes.
## Follow-up
Meal and glucose education.
## Special populations
Older adults and kidney disease.
