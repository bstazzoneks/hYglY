<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type: postprandial.</strong> Missed meals may prolong risk.</aside>
## What it is
Short-acting medicines that stimulate insulin around meals.
## Quick summary
- Action follows meal timing.
- Skipped food increases risk.
- Repeated episodes may occur.
- Do not change timing alone.
- Measurements should coincide with symptoms.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, hunger and confusion.
## Why it happens
Medicine may release insulin when food provides insufficient glucose.
## Who may have it
People with diabetes using meglitinides.
## How it is diagnosed
Review timing and show critical glucose or elevated insulin.
## Treatment
The team adjusts timing or changes medicine.
## Food and eating
Take food according to the advised plan. Avoid skipped meals.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose and recheck after 15 minutes.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Do not drive with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Do episodes follow skipped meals?
- Do I need timing adjustment?
- Which glucose level should I watch?
