## What it is
The immune system damages adrenal glands and lowers cortisol.
## Quick summary
- Low glucose may occur.
- Weakness and low blood pressure are common.
- Nausea and weight loss may occur.
- Treatment requires corticosteroids.
- Do not stop hormones without advice.
- Stress can cause crisis.
## Symptoms
Fatigue, dizziness, nausea, dark skin and confusion.
## Why it happens
Low cortisol reduces counter-regulatory glucose response.
## Who may have it
People with thyroiditis, type 1 diabetes or other autoimmune disease.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose, cortisol, ACTH and electrolytes.
## Treatment
Adrenal replacement and emergency plan.
## Food and eating
Regular meals and hydration.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose, take rapid glucose if low and follow the cortisol plan.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for vomiting, dehydration, low pressure or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Carry a medical card and sick-day plan.
## Questions for your doctor
- Do I have adrenal antibodies?
- Do I need stress-dose cortisol?
- Which plan applies to hypoglycemia?
