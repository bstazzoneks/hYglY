## Clinical summary
Glucocorticoid deficiency with hypoglycemia risk.
## Definition
Autoimmune damage to adrenal cortex.
## Epidemiology
Major autoimmune cause of adrenal insufficiency in adults.
## Pathophysiology
Low cortisol reduces hepatic glucose production and counter-regulation.
## Clinical presentation
Hypotension, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, nausea and hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low cortisol with high ACTH and antibodies when appropriate.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Cortisol and ACTH | Confirms insufficiency |
| ACTH stimulation | Assesses reserve |
| Electrolytes | Detects associated abnormalities |

## Differential diagnosis
Hypopituitarism, sepsis, dehydration and medicines.
## Management
Glucocorticoid replacement and crisis care.
## Complications and prognosis
Adrenal crisis and death if untreated.
## Follow-up
Dosing, associated disease and sick-day plan.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
