<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Document low glucose and seek a secondary mechanism.
## Definition
Obesity alone does not define hypoglycemia.
## Epidemiology
Associated hypoglycemia is poorly characterized.
## Pathophysiology
Tissue resistance and compensatory hyperinsulinism.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial or nonspecific symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple's triad with simultaneous measurement.
## Tests and cutoff values
No dedicated values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses hyperinsulinism |
| Surgical history | Excludes dumping or post-bariatric disease |

## Differential diagnosis
Dumping, medicines, insulinoma and anxiety.
## Management
Nutrition goals, activity and metabolic control.
## Complications and prognosis
Food restriction and neuroglycopenic injury if hypoglycemia is real.
## Follow-up
Monitor weight, glucose and recurrence.
## Special populations
Pregnancy, bariatric surgery and older adults.
