## Clinical summary
Rare tumor hypoglycemia from hormonal or metabolic origin.
## Definition
Mechanism not included under insulinoma or IGF-2.
## Epidemiology
Poorly defined.
## Pathophysiology
Insulin, IGF, low glucagon or altered metabolism.
## Clinical presentation
Varies by tumor.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and hormonal profile plus imaging.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Insulin and C-peptide | Endogenous origin |
| IGF | Non-islet hypoglycemia |
| Imaging | Locate tumor |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, IGF-2, medicines and liver disease.
## Management
Tumor control and glucose support.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and tumor progression.
## Follow-up
Oncology and endocrinology.
## Special populations
Advanced cancer.
