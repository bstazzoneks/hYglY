## What it is
A cautious category for non-pancreatic tumors with rare mechanisms.
## Quick summary
- Low glucose must be documented.
- Insulin, IGF and C-peptide help.
- Commoner causes must be excluded.
- Treatment depends on tumor.
- This category alone does not confirm tumor.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, confusion or loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Endogenous insulin, IGF or metabolic alteration may occur.
## Who may have it
People with known mass or advanced cancer.
## How it is diagnosed
Critical sample, imaging, insulin, C-peptide and IGF testing.
## Treatment
Tumor control and specialized glucose support.
## Food and eating
Frequent meals during evaluation.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which hormone explains episodes?
- Which tumor is likely?
- Which treatment may control glucose?
## More information
Specialized oncology and endocrine teams.
