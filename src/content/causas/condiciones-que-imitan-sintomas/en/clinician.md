## Clinical summary
Differential diagnosis of autonomic symptoms.
## Definition
Conditions mimicking trembling, sweating or dizziness.
## Epidemiology
Not quantified.
## Pathophysiology
Autonomic, rhythm or catecholamine pathways.
## Clinical presentation
Palpitations, dizziness, anxiety or headache.
## Diagnostic criteria
Valid simultaneous measurement and alternative tests.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms hypoglycemia |
| ECG or Holter | Assess arrhythmia |
| Orthostatic pressure | Assess POTS |
| Metanephrines | Assess pheochromocytoma |
## Differential diagnosis
True hypoglycemia, anxiety and syncope.
## Management
Treat confirmed alternative.
## Complications and prognosis
Depends on diagnosis.
## Follow-up
Cardiology, endocrinology or mental health as indicated.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and older adults.
