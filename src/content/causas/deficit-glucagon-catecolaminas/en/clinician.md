## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from limited counter-regulation.
## Definition
Glucagon or catecholamine deficit in a defined clinical context.
## Epidemiology
Rare and not quantified.
## Pathophysiology
Liver loses rapid signals for glucose release.
## Clinical presentation
Hypoglycemia with variable symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose and assessment of pancreatic, autonomic or pituitary injury.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Glucagon response | Assesses reserve |
| Counter-regulatory hormones | Supports mechanism |
| Autonomic testing | Assesses neuropathy |

## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, medicines, Addison disease and hypopituitarism.
## Management
Treat primary cause and prevent episodes.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and rare death.
## Follow-up
Monitor episodes and comorbidity.
## Special populations
Pediatric care and older adults.
