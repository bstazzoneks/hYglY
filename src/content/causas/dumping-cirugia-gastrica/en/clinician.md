<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: both.</strong> Early dumping is not hypoglycemia. Late dumping may be true postprandial hypoglycemia.</aside>

## Clinical summary
Dumping follows accelerated gastric emptying and hormone changes after gastric surgery.

Late dumping requires simultaneous low glucose to confirm hypoglycemia.

## Definition
Early dumping follows rapid movement of stomach contents. It produces vasomotor and gastrointestinal symptoms.

Late dumping may include excessive insulin secretion.

## Epidemiology
Frequency varies by operation and technique. It follows bypass, gastrectomy and procedures altering pyloric or vagal function.

## Pathophysiology
Intestinal volume and hormones change rapidly in early dumping. In late dumping, incretins may intensify insulin.

## Clinical presentation
| Type | Timing | Findings |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Within one hour | Nausea, diarrhea, sweating, palpitations |
| Late | One to three hours | Trembling, confusion, low glucose |

## Diagnostic criteria
Confirm type by timing and physiology. For late dumping, apply Whipple's triad and measure glucose.

## Tests and cutoff values
No cutoff values are listed without a cited guideline in this record.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Mixed-meal test | Characterizes postprandial response |
| Serum glucose | Confirms late hypoglycemia |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses endogenous hyperinsulinism |
| Supervised diet | Differentiates rapid absorption symptoms |

## Differential diagnosis
Post-bariatric hypoglycemia, insulinoma, gastroparesis, sulfonylureas and early diabetes.

## Management
Small portions, liquid separation and carbohydrate adjustment. Consider medicines or surgical review if persistent.

## Complications and prognosis
Dehydration, malnutrition, falls and neuroglycopenic events. Prognosis depends on surgery and management.

## Follow-up
Document timing, glucose and dietary response. Refer for unexplained episodes.

## Special populations
Adapt for pregnancy, pediatric patients and older adults.
