## What it is
Pituitary inflammation that may lower ACTH, TSH or other hormones.
## Quick summary
- Hypoglycemia may occur.
- Headache or visual change may occur.
- Immunotherapy may contribute.
- Hormone and imaging tests are needed.
- Do not stop hormone replacement.
- Management may include immunosuppression.
## Symptoms
Weakness, nausea, headache, visual change and confusion.
## Why it happens
ACTH or cortisol deficit lowers counter-regulation.
## Who may have it
People with autoimmune disease or immunotherapy exposure.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose, cortisol, ACTH, TSH and other hormones. Use pituitary imaging when indicated.
## Treatment
Hormone replacement and neuroendocrine review.
## Food and eating
Regular meals without prolonged fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Measure glucose and take rapid glucose if low.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for severe headache, vision loss or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Carry a hormone plan and avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Which hormones are low?
- Did immunotherapy trigger it?
- Do I need imaging or biopsy?
## More information
Neuroendocrine team.
