## Clinical summary
Hypothalamic-pituitary deficit with possible hypoglycemia.
## Definition
Primary or immunotherapy-associated pituitary inflammation.
## Epidemiology
Rare.
## Pathophysiology
ACTH deficit lowers cortisol and counter-regulation.
## Clinical presentation
Headache, visual change and hormone symptoms.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose, low cortisol, ACTH assessment and pituitary imaging.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary cutoff values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Cortisol and ACTH | Confirms deficit |
| Pituitary MRI | Assesses mass or inflammation |
| Pituitary hormones | Documents multiple deficits |

## Differential diagnosis
Macroadenoma, pituitary apoplexy, sepsis and isolated Addison disease.
## Management
Hormone replacement and immunotherapy review.
## Complications and prognosis
Persistent hormone deficit and crisis.
## Follow-up
Monitor adrenal axis and vision.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and immunotherapy.
