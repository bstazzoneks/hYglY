## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from administered insulin.
## Definition
Relative or absolute excess of exogenous insulin.
## Epidemiology
Main pharmacologic cause in treated diabetes.
## Pathophysiology
Peripheral uptake and hepatic suppression.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial, nocturnal or exercise-related.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose, compatible insulin and suppressed C-peptide according to context.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Insulin and C-peptide | Exclude endogenous origin |
| Administration history | Confirms exposure |
## Differential diagnosis
Sulfonylurea, insulinoma and factitious hypoglycemia.
## Management
Adjust dose, type and education.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and recurrence.
## Follow-up
Monitoring and safety.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
