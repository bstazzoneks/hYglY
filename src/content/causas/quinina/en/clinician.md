## Clinical summary
Drug-induced hypoglycemia from insulin stimulation.
## Definition
Quinine exposure with documented hypoglycemia.
## Epidemiology
Rare, reported in cases.
## Pathophysiology
Stimulated insulin secretion.
## Clinical presentation
Persistent or recurrent episodes.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose and confirmed exposure.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Insulin and C-peptide | Confirm origin |
| Drug screening | Exclude other medicines |
## Differential diagnosis
Sulfonylureas, insulinoma and alcohol.
## Management
Supervised withdrawal.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenic injury.
## Follow-up
Observation until remission.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and older adults.
