## What it is
Quinine may stimulate insulin and cause hypoglycemia.
## Quick summary
- Repeated episodes may occur.
- Exposure should be stopped if directed.
- Critical glucose is measured.
- Insulin and C-peptide may rise.
- Only medical use should guide exposure.
## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, confusion and seizures.
## Why it happens
Drug-related insulin stimulation.
## Who may have it
People exposed to quinine.
## How it is diagnosed
Exposure history, critical glucose and hormone testing.
## Treatment
Supervised withdrawal and glucose support.
## Food and eating
Regular meals and rapid glucose.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able and recheck.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms.
## Questions for your doctor
- Did I take quinine?
- Was exposure confirmed?
- Do I need prolonged observation?
