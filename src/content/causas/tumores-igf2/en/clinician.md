## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from large tumors producing IGF-2.
## Definition
Non-islet cell hypoglycemia.
## Epidemiology
Rare, associated with mesenchymal or hepatic masses.
## Pathophysiology
IGF-2 activates insulin-like signaling and peripheral uptake.
## Clinical presentation
Recurrent fasting hypoglycemia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Low glucose with suppressed insulin and C-peptide; seek mass and IGF.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Insulin and C-peptide | Suggest non-islet origin |
| IGF-1 and IGF-2 | Specialized support |
| Whole-body imaging | Locate tumor |
## Differential diagnosis
Insulinoma, medicines, liver and adrenal disease.
## Management
Resection; glucose support if persistent.
## Complications and prognosis
Neuroglycopenia and tumor recurrence.
## Follow-up
Oncology, imaging and glucose.
## Special populations
Advanced cancer.
