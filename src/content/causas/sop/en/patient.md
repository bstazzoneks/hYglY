<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong> PCOS alone does not confirm hypoglycemia.</aside>

## What it is
PCOS affects cycles, androgen levels and metabolism. Insulin resistance may increase postprandial responses.

## Quick summary
- Hyperinsulinism may occur.
- Symptoms require measurement.
- Pregnancy requires adaptation.
- Medicines and fasting require review.
- Activity improves sensitivity.
- Do not start restrictive diets alone.

## Symptoms
Trembling, sweating, hunger or confusion after eating.

## Why it happens
Tissue responds less to insulin. The pancreas may produce more to maintain glucose.

## Who may have it
People with PCOS, obesity or metabolic family history.

## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose during symptoms. Review PCOS criteria and exclude medicine-related hypoglycemia.

## Treatment
Metabolic changes, activity and hormone management when indicated. Avoid unsupervised medicines.

## Food and eating
Regular meals with measured carbohydrate and fiber.

## What to do during an episode
If conscious and glucose is low, take rapid glucose and recheck after 15 minutes.

## When to seek emergency care
Seek care for loss of consciousness, seizures or persistent confusion.

## Daily life
Avoid driving with symptoms. Plan exercise and alcohol with the team.

## Questions for your doctor
- Was low glucose confirmed?
- What relation may there be to PCOS?
- Do I need metabolic tests?

## More information
Gynecology and endocrine specialists.
