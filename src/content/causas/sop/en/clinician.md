<aside class="medical-type"><strong>Type of hypoglycemia produced most often: postprandial.</strong></aside>
## Clinical summary
Assess hyperinsulinism without overdiagnosing hypoglycemia.
## Definition
PCOS requires gynecologic and endocrine diagnostic criteria.
## Epidemiology
Specific hypoglycemia prevalence is unquantified.
## Pathophysiology
Peripheral resistance and compensatory hyperinsulinism.
## Clinical presentation
Postprandial symptoms and metabolic signs.
## Diagnostic criteria
Whipple's triad with simultaneous measurement.
## Tests and cutoff values
No dedicated values are listed.

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms hypoglycemia |
| Insulin and C-peptide | Assesses hyperinsulinism |
| PCOS studies | Establish context |

## Differential diagnosis
Anxiety, dumping, medicines and early diabetes.
## Management
Activity, nutrition and gynecoendocrine treatment.
## Complications and prognosis
Food restriction and metabolic risk.
## Follow-up
Monitor glucose and reproductive health.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and contraception.
