## What it is
Insufficient intake lowers energy and liver reserves.
## Quick summary
- Fasting increases risk.
- Illness may worsen it.
- Nutrition should be restored gradually.
- Severe hypoglycemia needs care.
## Symptoms
Weakness, trembling, confusion and loss of consciousness.
## Why it happens
Glycogen and other fuels are insufficient.
## Who may have it
People with malnutrition, prolonged vomiting or malabsorption.
## How it is diagnosed
Measure glucose and assess nutrition, liver and endocrine causes.
## Treatment
Supervised nutrition restart and cause correction.
## Food and eating
Gradual meals according to team. Avoid fasting.
## What to do during an episode
Take rapid glucose if able.
## When to seek emergency care
Seek help for seizures or loss of consciousness.
## Daily life
Ask for nutrition support and avoid driving with symptoms.
