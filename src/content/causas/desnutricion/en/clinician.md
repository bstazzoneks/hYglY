## Clinical summary
Hypoglycemia from exhausted reserves.
## Definition
Prolonged inadequate intake.
## Epidemiology
Associated with malnutrition and vomiting.
## Pathophysiology
Glycogen, fat and gluconeogenesis are insufficient.
## Clinical presentation
Weakness and neuroglycopenia.
## Diagnostic criteria
Critical glucose and nutrition assessment.
## Tests and cutoff values
No proprietary values are listed.
| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Critical glucose | Confirms |
| Liver function | Assess reserve |
| Electrolytes | Guide refeeding |
## Differential diagnosis
Endocrine disease, alcohol and metabolic disease.
## Management
Supervised gradual nutrition.
## Complications and prognosis
Refeeding syndrome and cerebral injury.
## Follow-up
Nutrition and underlying cause.
## Special populations
Pregnancy and pediatric care.
